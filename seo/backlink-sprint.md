# Backlink Sprint Playbook

## Goal
Acquire relevant links to hubs, tools, and companion guides without aggressive outreach volume.

## Priority Link Targets
1. Parenting newsletters and resource roundups.
2. Pediatric and family-health directories that list tools.
3. Podcast show notes related to first-year parenting.
4. Local-language parenting communities (ES/FR/PT) with curated resources.

## Asset-to-Target Mapping
- Tool pages: directory/tool roundup placements.
- Pillar hubs: long-form roundup references.
- Printable checklists: newsletter and community resource links.

## Outreach Workflow (Weekly)
1. Identify 25 prospects per locale.
2. Match one relevant Raffy asset per prospect.
3. Send personalized outreach (value-first, not template-only).
4. Track status (`sent`, `replied`, `won`, `declined`) in a shared sheet.
5. Re-contact after 7-10 days with a lighter follow-up.

## Quality Rules
- Prioritize topical relevance over raw domain metrics.
- Avoid paid/spam placements.
- Keep anchors natural and language-localized.

## KPI
- Weekly: new referring domains by locale.
- Monthly: link velocity to hub + tool cluster pages.
