# SEO Traffic Scorecard (90-Day)

Use this sheet every Monday for EN/ES/FR/PT.

## Baseline Snapshot (Week 0)
- Export GSC by locale folder (`/en/`, `/es/`, `/fr/`, `/pt/`).
- Capture: clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, top non-brand queries, top non-brand pages.
- Save exports under `/seo/reports/` (CSV).

## Weekly Review
- Non-brand clicks (by locale)
- Non-brand impressions (by locale)
- New URLs receiving first clicks (count)
- Tool pages in Top 20 (count)
- Companion pages in Top 20 (count)

## 30-Day Checkpoint
- Indexation growth for newly published pages.
- Number of new pages with any impressions.
- Number of new pages with any clicks.

## 60-Day Checkpoint
- % of new pages in Top 20.
- Tool pages entering Top 10 / Top 20.
- Internal-link assisted pages with positive impression trend.

## 90-Day Acceptance Targets
- +40% non-brand impressions across EN/ES/FR/PT combined.
- At least 12 new pages per locale in Top 20.
- At least 4 tool or companion pages with steady non-brand clicks.
- 100% of health-related articles include trust block + sources + Article schema.
