# Off-site mentions: outreach target list (T-022)

Internal doc, not published. Date checked: 2026-10-31. Outreach is done manually by the owner; no paid placements.

**Expect a low reply rate from list owners (~5-15%).** Send few, personal, value-first messages; do not mass-send.

Product facts used in all copy come from `LLMS.md` and `en/index.html` (app languages from the owner's ticket brief): Raffy is an Android-only baby care app (sleep, feeding, growth vs WHO curves, medicines, doctor visits), free on Google Play, app languages en/es/fr/pt (NOT it/de). The website has free tools and articles in en/es/fr/pt/it/de. For it/de targets pitch the free web tools and articles first, not the app.

Play Store: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gurrulabs.raffy

Research notes:
- Targets were opened via fetch on 2026-10-31. Dead and 2025-inactive ones were dropped (see "Dropped / could not open" at the end).
- Ranking/citation for "best baby tracker app" and local equivalents was not measurable with the tools used. raffyparenting.com did not appear in the roundups opened. Run a Search Console check for these queries before judging results.

## (c) Reddit and community guidance

- Read the community's rules (sidebar/wiki) before posting; many ban or limit app promotion and some require mod approval or a flair.
- Disclose affiliation every time you mention Raffy: "I'm the developer of Raffy."
- Answer the question first, with real information (and a pediatrician caveat for health topics). Add a link only if asked or clearly useful.
- Keep roughly a 9:1 ratio of non-promotional contributions to any mention of Raffy; build a normal history first.
- Never ask for votes, upvotes, reviews or "shares", and never ask friends or colleagues to do so.
- One account only, in your own name or clearly yours; no sock puppets, no fake recommendations.
- Say it is Android-only and free when you mention it; for it/de communities mention only the free web tools.
- Stop if a mod asks you to; do not repost removed content.
