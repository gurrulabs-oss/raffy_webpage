# Raffy Web art style (drafted 2026-11-17 from existing article covers)

Article covers are photorealistic lifestyle photos, not illustrations.

- Format: landscape 3:2 (exported to 1200×630, so keep the subject in the central horizontal band, nothing important near top/bottom edges).
- Look: soft natural light, shallow depth of field, calm and warm, real-looking skin and fabric; no stock-photo grin, no glossy ad look.
- Palette: warm neutrals (cream #F4EEE6, oat #E6DACB, light oak wood #C9A27A), soft sage green #A9BFA0, muted baby blue #B9CCDD; small accents only.
- Setting: tidy Scandinavian-style home, light wood furniture, linen, knitted blankets, a plant, a plush toy.
- People: babies the focus; parents often partly in frame (hands, torso) or faces soft; diverse but unremarkable.
- Never: text, logos, watermarks, phone screens with UI, brand products.
- Safety: babies shown in safe positions (safe sleep: on back, firm flat surface, no loose pillows near a sleeping baby; no baby asleep on a sofa).
