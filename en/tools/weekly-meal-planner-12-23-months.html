Who this weekly meal planner helps

This page is built for parents searching for a weekly meal planner for a 1-year-old, a 7-day toddler meal plan, or a baby menu for 12 to 23 months. It generates a full week of breakfast, snacks, lunch, and dinner, then checks calories, milk targets, and weekly variety.

Use it when you want a practical toddler meal routine for daycare weeks, family meals, picky phases, or a more structured menu for babies moving into the second year.

How the 7-day baby menu is built

  • Breakfast and dinner include the age-based milk target for the day.
  • Every generated week includes 5 meals per day and all 35 meal slots.
  • The planner checks calorie range, weekly macro balance, repetition limits, and WHO or pediatric safety rules.
  • The menu is randomized on each run so parents can create new weekly ideas without repeating the same pattern.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a meal planner for 1-year-old babies and kids?

Yes. This tool is designed for babies and toddlers aged 12 to 23 months.

Does the menu enforce weekly variety?

Yes. The planner applies weekly repetition limits and a minimum unique-dishes threshold.

Is this a medical diagnosis tool?

No. It is an educational planning tool based on public guidance and does not replace pediatric advice.
