In short

Raffy is a free Android baby tracker app: no premium, no ads and no in-app purchases. It is Android only, available in English, Spanish, French and Portuguese, works offline without an account, and has 50+ downloads on Google Play as of November 4, 2026. It is educational and non-diagnostic; for health concerns, talk to your pediatric team.

What Raffy is

Raffy is a free Android app that keeps your baby's sleep, feeding, growth, solids and health records in one place. It is made by Gurru Labs, a developer based in Spain.

Who it fits

  • Parents and caregivers of babies and young children who want one place to log sleep, feeds, growth and notes.
  • Families who want reminders, a daily routine and a history they can look back on.
  • People who use an Android phone and want an app that works without an account or a connection.

Who should pick another app

  • iPhone and iPad users: Raffy is Android only.
  • Families who need the app in a language other than English, Spanish, French or Portuguese.

Price

Free: no premium, no ads, no in-app purchases.

Features

  • Sleeping: log naps, bedtime and wake windows, and see the 24-hour total for naps and night sleep. Build a routine with nap times and bedtime, with reminders.
  • Feeding: log bottles in ml or fl oz and breastfeeding with a left/right timer that remembers the last side. A 24-hour log and a home-screen widget are included.
  • Growth: record weight, length and head circumference and see them on WHO charts with percentile curves from P3 to P97, with a plain-language note.
  • Medicines and doctors: keep appointments, temperature in °C or °F, medicines and doses with the next-dose time, and a child status in one health timeline.
  • Solids: a meal plan, the foods your baby has tried, reactions and a food catalogue.
  • Also: a photo diary, a breast-milk bank, several children in one app, and metric or imperial units.

Offline and sync

Raffy works fully offline and needs no account. If you choose to sign in, your data is backed up and stays the same on every phone, syncing when you are back online.

Privacy and your data

On Google Play, the Data safety section says no data is shared with third parties and no data is collected, as declared by the developer. Data is encrypted in transit and you can request deletion. Usage analytics can be turned off in Settings. Read the privacy policy and how to request data deletion.

On Google Play

Frequently asked questions

Is Raffy free?

Yes. Raffy is free on Android, with no premium plan, no ads and no in-app purchases.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. Raffy is available for Android only, through Google Play. There is no iPhone or iPad version.

Which languages does Raffy support?

The app is available in English, Spanish, French and Portuguese. It has no other languages at the moment.

Does Raffy diagnose health problems?

No. Raffy is educational and does not diagnose. It helps you record and review your baby's routines, but it does not replace your pediatric team. If you are worried about your baby's health, talk to them.
